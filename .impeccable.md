## Design Context

### Users
High-performing professionals aged 25–45, evaluating RESET90 for themselves or on behalf of their company. They browse on phones between meetings, on laptops in offices, and on desktop monitors at home. They are results-oriented, skeptical of generic fitness promises, and calibrate trust through visual signals of quality. Corporate decision-makers may also land on this site — the design must read as credible to a company, not just an individual.

### Brand Personality
Premium, earned, precise.

Not aspirational-generic. The brand feels like it has already proven itself — it doesn't shout for attention. Think Hublot or Tom Ford: dark, deliberate, gold-accented, spare. Every word and every pixel earns its place.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark (near-black backgrounds). The product is used in gym/performance contexts — evening sessions, early mornings, disciplined environments. Dark is the right choice.
- **Palette**: Black base (`#0a0a0a`), gold accent (`#c8a84e`). Muted, not flashy — the gold feels earned rather than decorative.
- **Typography**: Montserrat — geometric, disciplined, readable at all weights.
- **Motion**: Framer Motion scroll reveals. Purposeful, not decorative.
- **Anti-references**: Generic fitness apps (flashy neons, stock-photo hero with bold sans). Budget coaching sites. Anything that feels mass-market.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint signals quality.** Less is more. White space and silence communicate premium better than decoration.
2. **Every breakpoint must feel intentional.** The design was built on a 27" monitor — it must adapt gracefully to 13" laptops, tablets, and phones without degrading.
3. **Corporate credibility.** Company stakeholders may view this. The site must look like a professional services brand, not a personal trainer's Instagram page.
4. **Performance-first UX.** The target user is time-poor. Navigation must be immediate and friction-free on any device.
5. **Touch-first mobile.** Primary mobile entry points (CTA, nav links) must have adequate touch targets (44×44px minimum).
